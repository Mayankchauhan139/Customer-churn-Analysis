# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Using Python and Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer retention is a major challenge for businesses, especially in subscription-based industries. Losing existing customers, commonly known as **customer churn**, can significantly impact revenue and long-term business growth.

This project analyzes customer data to identify the key factors associated with customer churn. Using Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques, the project explores customer demographics, account information, service usage, contract types, payment methods, and billing patterns to uncover customer segments that are more likely to leave.

The goal of this analysis is to transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights that can help organizations improve customer retention and reduce churn.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The objective of this project is to understand:

* Which customers are most likely to churn?
* Which factors are strongly associated with churn?
* Which customer segments should be prioritized for retention strategies?

## 📈 Key Findings

* Customers with **month-to-month contracts** show higher churn risk than customers with longer-term contracts.
* **Newer customers** are more likely to churn, highlighting the importance of early customer engagement.
* Customers using **electronic check payments** represent a higher-risk segment.
* Customer churn varies across different service, billing, and customer segments.
* Monthly charges and contract characteristics are important factors to consider when analyzing churn behavior.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses could:

* Develop targeted retention campaigns for high-risk customer segments.
* Improve onboarding and engagement during the early customer lifecycle.
* Encourage customers to adopt longer-term contracts through incentives.
* Investigate the reasons behind higher churn among electronic check users.
* Provide personalized offers based on customer risk profiles.
